· HYP_RAM_AIRLINK_01: Degraded solutions to face any failure are supposed to be set on
efficiently. The risk associated to a lack of cars is not taken into account in this calculation.
· HYP_RAM_AIRLINK_02: The failures resulting from the following events are excluded:
o Dysfunction or suspension of the exploitation due to vandalism, “acts of god”, a misuse of the
system by the passengers, or induced by a passenger,
o Dysfunction or suspension of the exploitation due to a bad operation of the operators,
o Activities of maintenance of the fixed equipment approved by the Customer,
o Suspension of the exploitation due to a system intrusion caused by a person, an animal or an
object external with the system,
o Suspension of the exploitation due to an external cause with the system, for example:
§ The loss of the external Power Supply,
§ Fire alarms warning or the failures of the fire detection system
§ Instructions or directives of the police services, safety services…,
§ Cases of major forces,
§ Weather conditions not allowing the continuation of the operation,
§ Suspension of the exploitation for particular training (for example, training of the first-aid
organizations for the evacuation of a car).
· HYP_RAM_AIRLINK_03: When a failure occurs in a redundant on board ATC sub-system
(without impacting the passenger service), then the train service is maintained in degraded
solution. We assume that the train is sent to the workshop when it reaches the end of the line
and is replaced by another train. This assumption allows defining the MDT for the onboard radio
This hypothesis is only linked to the Radio System that is part of the CBTC solution, not related
to the Multimedia System.
· HYP_RAM_AIRLINK_04: About availability calculations only:
o Failures of the CSR, AP dongles and TU encryption cards are not taken into account because
they have no functional impact during nominal operating. In fact, dongles/encryption cards are
mainly used at boot time but availability calculations don't take into account any re/boot failure.
Dongle/encryption card are also used during operation in order to increase the entropy of
random numbers required by the software. Nevertheless the entropy achieved during a MDT
period without dongle is considered sufficient.
ð This last assumption is not applied to reliability calculations.

4.8. WAYSIDE TRANSPONDER
4.8.1. WAYSIDE TRANSPONDER FUNCTION AND ARCHITECTURE
DIGISAFE transponders are used to provide an absolute localisation reference to the on-board TGMT
computer when passing over a transponder. This reference is used by the Carborne Controller to
initialize the localisation of a non-localized train (i.e. a train exiting the non-automatic zone of the depot)
and to regularly reduce the localization uncertainty of an already localized train.
Transponders are robust, sturdy, passive components not requiring any power supply or cable
connection.
Position of the transponders on the railway network is determined during the detail design according to
the system performances constraints (i.e.: specific pattern of transponders at platform to improve ATO
stopping accuracy and safely open the platform doors).
