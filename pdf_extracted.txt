4.8.  WAYSIDE TRANSPONDER

4.8.1.  WAYSIDE TRANSPONDER FUNCTION AND ARCHITECTURE

DIGISAFE transponders are used to provide an absolute localisation reference to the on-board TGMT
computer  when  passing  over  a  transponder.  This  reference  is  used  by  the  Carborne  Controller  to
initialize the localisation of a non-localized train (i.e. a train exiting the non-automatic zone of the depot)
and to regularly reduce the localization uncertainty of an already localized train.

Transponders  are  robust,  sturdy,  passive  components  not  requiring  any  power  supply  or  cable
connection.

Position of the transponders on the railway network is determined during the detail design according to
the system performances constraints (i.e.: specific pattern of transponders at platform to improve ATO
stopping accuracy and safely open the platform doors).
